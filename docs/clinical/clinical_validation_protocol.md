# Clinical Validation Protocol: Dynamic Distress Score (DDS)

## Objective
To rigorously evaluate the sensitivity and specificity of the composite Dynamic Distress Score (DDS) against licensed psychologist gold-standard evaluations before fixed weights are operationalized.

## Cohort Design
- Retrospective Cohort: 2,500 anonymized historical helpline transcripts.
- Prospective Cohort: 500 consented participants across 5 pilot districts.

## Target Statistical Metrics
- Sensitivity: >= 0.85 for acute threat & crisis detection.
- Specificity: >= 0.80 to prevent unnecessary or traumatic intervention false positives.
